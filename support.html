Family Concierge - Support

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Family Concierge is a private app for a single two-person household. It keeps both partners' calendars, shared tasks, and day notes together in one calm daily page.

Getting help

For any question, problem, or feedback, email hello@familyconcierge.app. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Use the email and password for your account. If you forget your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen to receive a reset code by email.
  • How do I connect a calendar? Open Settings and connect your Google or Outlook account. You can disconnect it at any time, which removes the app's access.
  • How do I add an event, task, or note? Use the add button on any tab, or use voice and natural-language capture (for example, "dentist Tuesday 3pm").
  • How do I share with my partner? From Settings, invite your partner by email. When they sign up with that email, they join your household automatically.
  • How do I delete my data? Email us at the address above to request account and data deletion.
