Family Concierge - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Family Concierge is a private application built for a single two-person household. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the general public. It exists to help one couple keep their calendars, tasks, and notes in one place. This policy explains what the app handles and how that information is treated.

Who runs this app

The app is operated privately by its owner for personal household use. For any question about this policy or your data, contact itsabouttimema@gmail.com.

What information the app handles

  • Account information. Your name, email address, and a securely hashed password used to sign in.
  • Calendar data. If you connect a Google or Outlook account, the app reads your calendar events so it can show them in one unified view. This access is granted by you through Google or Microsoft sign-in and can be revoked at any time.
  • Your content. Tasks, day notes, and any text or voice input you create in the app.
  • Voice capture. When you use voice entry, speech is converted to text so it can be turned into an event, task, or note. It is used only for that purpose.

How the information is used

  • To provide the app's core features: a unified calendar, shared tasks, and day notes.
  • To interpret natural-language and voice input into calendar, task, and note entries.
  • To send essential account email such as sign-in verification and password reset.

Your information is never sold, rented, or used for advertising. There is no third-party ad tracking and no analytics profiles are built about you.

Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in the app's own private backend database, accessible only to the household members who use the app and the owner who operates it. A small number of trusted service providers process data strictly to make the app work:

  • Google and Microsoft - to read the calendars you choose to connect.
  • The app's hosting provider - to run the backend and store your data.
  • An email provider - to deliver account and verification email.
  • An AI provider - to interpret natural-language input you submit. Only the text needed to complete that request is sent.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as the account is active. You can request that your account and associated data be deleted by emailing itsabouttimema@gmail.com. Disconnecting a calendar removes the app's ongoing access to it.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, access is protected by signed tokens, and connections use encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure, but the app is built to limit access to your information to those who need it.

Children

The app is intended for use by the adults of one household and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
